# Spec update 32 — Photo headers, Progress order, Quick start spacing (16 Nov)

Mockups: `mockups/34-t-train.png`, `34-t-goal.png`, `34-t-progress.png`, `34-t-quick.png` (together `34-tabs-compare.png`). Hein liked Meals' "Food this week" photo header and wants the same on other tabs.

## 1. Photo headers (Train, Pick a goal, Progress)
- Full-bleed photo, **the same 240 pt height on every tab (Meals included)**, title block at the same position, dark gradient at the bottom, white title block (small caps line, 30 pt title, one grey line), status bar and ⋯ in white.
- Train: gym interior with machines; title = program name; line = "3× a week · Advanced · Gym · with Lenaigh"; Start <today> stays the one primary button.
- Pick a goal: dumbbell rack or similar; program names unchanged (Lean and strong, Back to it, Bigger arms, Service ready, Move freely…). Each program row shows a picture of the equipment it uses (machine, cable, dumbbells, rack; a pull-up bar for bodyweight programs).
- Progress: kettlebells, plates or a rack; title "Your week".
- **Photos: no people, no faces (Hein, privacy).** Equipment and empty gyms only. Crop out or replace any photo where a person appears, even small or from behind. Remove the "Lean and strong" photo of a woman. Credits for every photo in docs/LICENCES/moments/CREDITS.md and Licences and credits. Hein may supply his own empty-gym photos under the same asset names.

## 2. Progress order
Week ring and summary → ALL STATS tiles (See all ›) → LAST 30 DAYS heat map → THIS WEEK (collapsible, Hide/Show) → NEXT WEEK (collapsible, collapsed by default). Collapse state remembered.

## 3. Time choices
20 · 30 · 45 · 60 · 75+ in Quick start and Surprise me. 75+ means "up to 90 if the day allows", never filler: set and exercise caps unchanged; adult main lifts rest 2:30 (3:00 on a strength goal); a shared-bar session counts taking turns. The preview shows the honest estimate rounded to 5 ("about 70 min"). Typical at Mid: 62–73 min alone, 81–88 with a shared bar, Full body 85–90.

## 4. Quick start sheet
Sections TRAINING WITH, FOCUS, TIME and the preview separated by hairlines with 14 pt padding; preview as a plain list, one exercise per line, a labelled block per person.

## 5. Push generation
Two presses (horizontal, vertical), a fly, a lateral raise and one triceps move; never two triceps isolations plus a front raise.
