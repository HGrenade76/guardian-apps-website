# TwoPlates app icon: illustrator brief ("Duo")

## The idea
Two people training together. In front, a man holding a loaded barbell at chest height. Just behind him and to the right, a woman flexing one arm. A gold ring sits behind them. Everything is warm gold on dark brushed graphite. The two plates on the bar are the name: TwoPlates.

Approved reference: `reference-v3-1024.jpg` (AI-generated by Hein, used only as a reference). `reference-v3-sizes.png` shows it at real iPhone sizes.

## What we need
An original redraw, not a trace, in the same composition and mood, as clean illustration:
1. **Composition:** keep the reference's layout. Pull both plates and the bar ends in about 3% so nothing touches the edge; keep at least 7% margin on every side for iOS corner rounding.
2. **Style:** bold illustrated shapes with gold shading, fewer fine lines than the reference (no hair strands, veins or skin texture), so it reads at 120 px and still recognisably at 58 px. Athletic, strong, not bodybuilder-extreme. Both faces equally lit, friendly and confident.
3. **Anatomy check:** clean hands (five fingers, natural grip), natural arm and shoulder masses.
4. **Faces:** invented, not resembling any real person.
5. **No text, no numbers, no frame.**

## Deliverables
- Layered source (Illustrator or Affinity), vector where possible.
- Icon Composer layers as separate PNG or SVG at 1024 × 1024: (a) background graphite with brushed texture and the gold ring, (b) the woman, (c) the man with the barbell.
- Flattened 1024 × 1024 PNG (sRGB, no transparency, no rounded corners) for the App Store.
- Dark and tinted checks (the layers must work when iOS tints them).
- Full transfer of copyright to Guardian Apps LLC (work for hire), in writing.

## Where it goes
App icon (iPhone, App Store), the website `assets/twoplates/tp-icon-512.webp` and share image, App Store screenshots.
