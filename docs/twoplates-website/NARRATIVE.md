# TwoPlates: narrative and copy for the coming-soon launch

For the website agent and for Hein. The prototype page (`prototype/twoplates.html`) already contains all the copy below; this file explains the thinking so edits stay on-message.

## 1. Positioning in one line

**TwoPlates: two people, one plan each, stronger together.**
A strength coach for you and the person you train with. Each of you gets a real program built for your own goal, and you train it side by side.

## 2. Who it's for (in this order)

1. **Training partners**: couples, friends, gym buddies. This is the launch audience.
2. **A parent bringing a teen (13 to 17) into the gym.** It's the emotional hook and the big difference from other apps, but never the only story: the buddy is "whoever you train with".
3. Never lead with "family app" or "couples app". Lead with *buddies*; family shows up where it's literal (teen rules, one household subscription).

## 3. The problem we name

Fitness apps are built for one person. People who stick with training usually have someone next to them. Today two people with different bodies and goals either do the same workout or train alone with separate apps.

## 4. The three things we promise (and only these)

| Promise | Proof on the page | Built today? |
|---|---|---|
| **One plan each** | Goal, kit (gym, home), days → a progressing program per person; Quick start | Yes |
| **Train together, live** | Join a session, whose turn, what to change on the bar, lock-screen timer, Apple Health | Yes (being polished in TestFlight build 2) |
| **Safe for teens by design** | No calories, weigh-ins, BMI or body-fat for teens; at least 2 reps in reserve; technique first; parent sees effort, not body numbers | Yes, enforced in the engine |

Supporting promise, inherited from Guardian Apps: **private by default** (your iCloud, no ads, no data selling, you choose what to share).

### Do not claim yet
- Logging a partner's sets on your phone (planned, not built).
- Garmin, Strava, Apple Watch, Android (on the roadmap only; FAQ says "when they're ready, not before").
- Pricing, launch date, "AI coach".
- Any result claims ("lose 10 lb", "double your strength").

## 5. Voice

Same as DECISIO's site: short sentences, plain words, confident, no hype. Add warmth: this is about people training together. Coach voice, not drill sergeant. Gym words people already use ("quick start", "reps in the tank", "what's on the bar").

## 6. Headlines

- Hero (chosen): **Two people. One plan each. *Stronger together.*** ("Stronger together" is on the app icon, so it ties together.)
- Alternates: "Train together. Progress apart." · "Your plan. Their plan. Same floor." · "Bring someone. Get stronger."
- Why section: **Fitness apps are built for one. People train in twos.**
- How it works: **Plan apart. Train together. See it add up.**
- Teens: **Bringing your teen? The rules are built in.**
- Closing: **Find your buddy. *Get on the list.***

Because it's presented as Hein's own words, he approves it or rewords it before launch. Options:
