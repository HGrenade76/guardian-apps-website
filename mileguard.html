Why this exists

Nobody remembers their miles in April.

If you drive for work, every business mile is worth money at tax time or on your expense claim. But only if it's written down: when, where, how far and why.

Most people rebuild the year from calendars and bank statements, or skip it. Tolls, parking and the client lunch get lost along the way.

MileGuard keeps that record as you go. It runs in the background, starts with your car, and asks one question when you park: business or personal?

Questions
When does MileGuard launch?

It's in beta with drivers now. Join the waitlist and you'll be the first to know, with an early invite to the beta if there's room.

Can I help test it?

Yes, please. Tap "Help us test" and send the short email with the email on your Apple account, and we'll send you a TestFlight invite.

Can I use it on my computer?

Yes, with Pro. Go to gap-apps.com/app and sign in with the Apple ID on your iPhone to review drives, add purposes and download your logs on a bigger screen. It reads straight from your own iCloud. If you use Advanced Data Protection, turn on "Access iCloud Data on the Web" in your iPhone's iCloud settings first.

Can I classify drives from the car?

Yes. Add the MileGuard widget to CarPlay (on your iPhone: Settings, General, CarPlay, your car, Widgets). After you park, it shows your last drive with Business and Personal buttons. The buttons work on touchscreen car displays and stay hidden while you're driving.

Do I need CarPlay?

No. MileGuard records drives on any iPhone. CarPlay or your car's Bluetooth just makes it sharper: drives start with the car, and rides as a passenger stay out.

Does it find my tolls?

It spots when a business drive used a toll road and flags it. Download the CSV statement from your toll account and import it; each toll is matched to its drive. You can also type the amount in.

How much will it cost?

MileGuard is free for 40 drives a month. Pro adds unlimited drives, PDF reports and the year-end summary, with a 2-week free trial. We'll confirm prices before launch.

Is this tax advice?

No. MileGuard keeps the records the IRS asks for and does the arithmetic. Your tax preparer decides what you claim.

Android?

MileGuard launches on iPhone with CarPlay. We'll announce other platforms when they're ready, not before.
