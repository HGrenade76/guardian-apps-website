Why this exists

Fitness apps are built for one. People train in twos.

Most people who stick with training have someone next to them: a partner on the bench, a friend who texts "you coming?", a parent bringing a teenager into the gym for the first time.

Every app we tried pretended the other person wasn't there. One plan, one goal, one phone. So two people with different bodies and different goals ended up doing the same workout, or doing it alone.

TwoPlates gives each of you a program that fits your goal, your equipment and your week, then puts you on the same floor: who's up next, what to change on the bar, and when it's your turn.

"Every app we tried assumed I trained alone. I didn't. So we built one for two."

Hein Ellis, founder, Guardian Apps

How it works

Plan apart. Train together. See it add up.

01

Your plan, built for you

Tell it your goal, your gym or home kit, and the days you can train. TwoPlates builds a program that progresses week to week, for each of you.

  • ✓ Strength, muscle, recomposition or general fitness
  • ✓ Gym, home, or both
  • ✓ Quick start when you just want to train today
02

Train side by side

Start a session together. Each of you sees your own sets and loads; the rest timer tells you whose turn it is and what to change on the bar.

  • ✓ One bar or two stations
  • ✓ Lock-screen timer and "your turn" alerts
  • ✓ Logs your workouts to Apple Health
03

Watch it add up

A weekly view of what you both did: sessions, streaks, personal records, and a 30-day map that makes consistency visible.

  • ✓ Weekly progress ring and streaks
  • ✓ PRs and "vs last time"
  • ✓ Nudge your buddy, not nag them
See every move

Every muscle, lit up.

Over 500 exercises, each with a looping 3D demonstration and the muscles it works highlighted in red. Not sure about your form between sets? Tap the exercise and watch it, slowed down if you like.

Safe for teens by design

Bringing your teen? The rules are built in.

Teens 13 to 17 join through a parent's household. Their experience is about technique, confidence and showing up, never about numbers on a body. These rules aren't settings. Nobody can switch them off, not even a parent.

No calories, no weigh-insTeens never see calories, deficits, goal weight, BMI or body-fat.
Reps in reserve, alwaysEvery set leaves at least two good reps in the tank. No max-out testing.
Technique firstMaster the rep range before the load goes up.
Parent as coach, not auditorParents see sessions and effort. Body numbers stay private to the teen.

Questions
When does TwoPlates launch?

We're testing it with families and training partners now. Join the waitlist and you'll be the first to know, with an early invite to the beta.

Can I help test it?

Yes, please. Tap "Help us test" and send the short email. Tell us the email on your Apple account and we'll send you a TestFlight invite. Testers need to be adults; if you train with a teen, the parent signs up for the household.

Do we both need an iPhone?

Yes, each of you uses TwoPlates on your own iPhone, so you each see your own plan, loads and timer while you train together.

Is it for couples, friends or families?

All of them. TwoPlates works for any two people who train together. Family features, like teen rules and one household subscription, switch on when you need them.

How old do you need to be?

13 and up. Teens 13 to 17 join through a parent's household, with built-in safety rules.

How much will it cost?

We'll share pricing before launch. Waitlist members hear first.

Android? Apple Watch? Garmin or Strava?

TwoPlates launches on iPhone with Apple Health. Watch and other connections are on the roadmap; we'll announce them when they're ready, not before.
