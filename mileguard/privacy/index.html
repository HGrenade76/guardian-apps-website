MileGuard Privacy Policy

Guardian Apps LLC · Effective November 22, 2026

MileGuard records your drives so you can keep a mileage log. Your drives stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account. We don't have accounts, we don't run ads, we don't use analytics or tracking, and we never sell your data.

What MileGuard collects and why

  • Location while you drive. MileGuard uses your iPhone's location to record the route and distance of each drive. It asks for "Always" access so drives are recorded when the app is closed. Location is only recorded during a drive, not while you're parked or walking.
  • Motion activity. Motion data tells MileGuard when you start and stop driving. It is used on your iPhone only and never leaves it.
  • What you enter. Trip purposes, clients, toll amounts, saved places, vehicles, odometer readings, your name and business name (for your reports), and your settings.
  • Toll statements you import. When you import a CSV from your toll provider, it is read on your iPhone to match tolls to drives. The file itself is not uploaded anywhere.

Where your data is kept

Your drives and everything you enter are stored on your iPhone. If you're signed in to iCloud, they are also synced to your private iCloud database so they survive a new phone and appear on your other devices. That database belongs to your Apple account. Guardian Apps cannot see or access it. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What is sent to our server

MileGuard uses a small Guardian Apps server for two things. Neither one involves an account, and nothing is linked to your name.

  • Toll check. For drives you mark as Business, the app sends the drive's route points (latitude, longitude and time) to our server to check whether the route used a toll road. Our server passes a sample of those points to the OpenStreetMap Overpass service (privacy policy) to look up toll roads. If toll pricing is enabled, the route may also be sent to Google's Routes API to estimate the toll amount (privacy policy). Routes are processed in memory to answer the request and are not stored by us. You can turn this off in Settings → Tolls.
  • In-car display. When a drive starts, the app sends our server a push token and the drive's start time so Apple's push service can show the drive on your lock screen and CarPlay Dashboard. The token is used for that one notification and is not stored.

Our server is hosted by Fly.io, whose systems may briefly keep standard request logs such as IP addresses for security and reliability. We do not use those logs to identify or profile anyone.

Purchases

MileGuard Pro is sold through Apple. Apple handles payment, and we never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation of whether your subscription is active.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts, no sign-in, no email collection.
  • No advertising, no ad networks, no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that send your data to third parties.
  • We never sell, rent or share your personal information for marketing.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays until you delete it. You can delete any drive in the app. Deleting the app removes the data on your iPhone. To remove the iCloud copy, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → MileGuard → Delete Data. Because we don't hold your drives on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete on our side, but you can contact us with any request.

Children

MileGuard is meant for adult drivers keeping business mileage records. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information is processed about you, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and to opt out of its sale. We don't sell personal information, and almost everything MileGuard handles is under your control on your own devices. Contact us for anything else and we'll respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and, for meaningful changes, let you know in the app.
