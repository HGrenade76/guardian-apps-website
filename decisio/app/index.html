Your meetings, on a bigger screen

See your actions, decisions and meeting records from any computer. Sign in with the Apple ID you use on your iPhone.

Can't sign in, or seeing "Authentication Error"?

If you use Advanced Data Protection, Apple turns off web access to your iCloud data. Turn it back on once and you're set. Everything stays end-to-end encrypted.

On your iPhone
  1. Open Settings
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap iCloud
  4. Scroll to the bottom and turn on Access iCloud Data on the Web
  5. Confirm with your passcode if asked
Or on your Mac
  1. Open System Settings
  2. Click your name at the top of the sidebar
  3. Click iCloud
  4. Turn on Access iCloud Data on the Web

Then come back here and sign in again. If your iPhone asks you to allow access, tap Allow.

Still stuck? Make sure you're signing in with the same Apple ID that's on your iPhone, and that iCloud is turned on for DECISIO (Settings → your name → iCloud → Saved to iCloud).

Your data is read from your own iCloud, in this browser. Guardian Apps never sees it.
